# Changelog

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-15

- Where an aircraft flies comes first: the tile, the card's list and the popup lead with its route ("Milan to Chicago", or its airports' codes where the cities do not fit), with its flight and airline under it, and the radar says where the nearest one goes. The photo comes higher: above the words wherever a tile has room for it, and right under the route in the popup. The count of aircraft moved to the end.

## 0.1.1 - 2026-11-15

- The count says how many aircraft the radar shows, within its range of the place, not how many the service sent.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-15

- The first version: a radar of the aircraft around your home, with the nearest one's photo and route, a card with
  every aircraft and a popup for each. Needs plugin API 0.27.
